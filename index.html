A New Standard in Modern Luxury Womenswear

Founded by Aniebiet King, Annieve & Co. is a luxury womenswear studio celebrated for its distinct pan-African aesthetic, precision draping, and pattern drafting excellence.

With over 11 years of experience across West Africa and Europe, Annieve & Co. offers an integrated approach to fashion design—combining bespoke occasion apparel with executive image consulting, personal branding, and sustainable circular design principles.
